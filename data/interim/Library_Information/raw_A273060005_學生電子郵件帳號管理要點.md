## Article 1 Purpose

The email accounts of the University's students (hereinafter referred to as the "Accounts") are provided for the University's students and alumni/alumnae to engage in academic research and interact with the school. The "Guidelines on Student Email Account Management" (hereinafter referred to as the "Guidelines") are formulated to expressly clarify the rights and obligations of users in accordance with the "Campus Network Usage Regulations".

## 第二條 適用範圍

凡符合以下身分者，經教務處或學生事務處校友服務組 （以下簡稱校服組）確認後，由圖書資訊處網路媒體組(以

下簡稱本組)建立電子郵件帳號：

- 一、 凡具本校正式學籍之在校學生。
- 二、 本校校友。
- 三、 具本校學籍之學生，於入學資料確認後，即以其學 號為電子郵件帳號統一建立，畢業後仍可延用且不 得以任何理由重覆申請。
- 四、 九十四學年度以前之校友，本校校服組制定之管理 規則確認身分後，統一由校服組提出申請。帳號一
- 律為在學時之學號。
- 五、 退學生視為已喪失本校學籍身分，故取消該帳號使
- 用權。

For those who meet the following status, after being confirmed by the Office of Academic Affairs or the Alumni Service Division, Office of Student Affairs (hereinafter referred to as the "School Service Division"), the Division of Network Management, Office of Library and Information

1. All students with official status of the University.

3. For a student with the status as the University's student, after the admission information is confirmed, his/her student number shall be used as an email account, which can be extended after graduation without being reapplied for any reason.

## 第四條 資料運用範圍

為便於教職員與學生連繫，學生電子郵件帳號，僅公佈於 校園入口網站內，供校內教職員查詢使用。

## Article 4 Scope of Data Use

Student email accounts are only published on the Campus Information Portal for inquiries made by faculty and staff in order to facilitate the communication between faculty/staff and students.

## 第五條 遵守要點

使用本帳號，必須遵守本要點、「校園網路使用規範」、教 育部訂定之「台灣學術網路使用規範」、以及本校所訂定 之相關使用規章辦法，與國家所制訂之各式法律規章，嚴 禁下列行為：

- 一、 從事商業行為與轉供他人使用。
- 二、 傳輸、放置、散播任何病毒、非法軟體、廣告信件 及牽涉色情、毀謗、威脅之內容。
- 三、 移動、修改、窺視任何不屬於個人所有之檔案及目 錄。
- 四、 破壞系統、干擾系統運作或從事任何影響系統安全
- 與入侵之行為。

## Article 5 Rules to be Followed

When using this account, the Rules of this Article, the "Campus Network Usage Regulations", the "Taiwan Academic Network Usage Regulations" stipulated by the Ministry of Education, and the relevant usage rules and regulations stipulated by the University, as well as various laws and regulations formulated by the Country shall be complied with. The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:

1. Engaging in business activities and transferring them to
2. others for use.
2. Transmitting, placing, and disseminating any viruses, illegal software, advertising mailings, as well as content involving pornography, slander, or threats.
3. Moving, modifying, and peeking at any files and directories that do not belong to you.
4. Destroying systems, interfering with system operation, or engaging in any behavior which affects system security as well as an intrusion.

## 第六條 處分原則

若違反相關規定，本組有權隨時停止該帳號使用，並依照 情節輕重，依校規處置。

## Article 6 Principle of Punishment

If relevant regulations are violated, this Division has the right to stop the use of the account at any time, and such violation shall be handled in accordance with school regulations depending on the seriousness of the case.

## 第七條 使用聲明

使用本帳號服務時，即表示已閱讀、瞭解並同意遵守本帳 號相關規範。

## Article 7 Statement of Use

Using this account service shall be deemed to have read, understood and agreed to abide by the regulations pertaining to this account.

## 第八條 實施與修訂

本要點經行政會議通過，陳請校長核定後公布實施，修訂 時亦同。

## Article 8 Implementation and Amendment

The Directions shall be passed by the University Administrative Meeting and will be enacted and become effective after the approval of the principal. The same shall apply to any amendments to the Procedures.
