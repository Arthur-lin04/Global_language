## Contents

第一條 目的

為使本校書刊及媒體資料之徵集、館藏管理及讀者服務有所遵循，以 達成提供一般教育、實務教學、專業學習所需之資訊，特訂定明志科 技大學圖書館管理作業規範(以下簡稱本規範)。

Article 1 Purpose

The Ming Chi University of Technology Work Specification for Library Management System (hereinafter referred to as "this Specification") is formulated to comply with the gathering, collection management and reader services of this School's books, periodicals and media materials, as well as to provide the information required for general education, practical teaching and professional learning.

第二條 適用範圍

凡本校圖書、非書資料、期刊、資料庫等資料之請購、登錄、分類編 目、館藏管理、讀者服務等相關事項，悉依本規範之規定辦理。

Article 2 Scope of Application

Matters related to the request and purchase, registration, classification and Cataloging, collection management, reader services and other matters of this School's books, non-book materials, periodicals, databases and other materials shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of this Specification.

## Article 4 Collection Scope

This School is a professional technical and vocational education institution; thus, this Library's collections mainly focus on natural and applied science, and partially based on humanities and social science. The collections in Chinese are required as complete as possible, and the collections in Western languages are required as refined as possible. When collecting information, its academic and practical reference value is given top priority.

為充實館藏，本館所徵集之圖書資訊型態可分為圖書、期刊、資料

庫、參考工具書及非書資料。

## 第六條 複本原則

為求有限的圖書經費，做廣泛有效的收藏，館藏資料以單本為原則，如 需複本時應考慮下列因素：

- 一、 資料之學術性、研究價值。
- 二、 資料新穎性。
- 三、 借閱頻率特高之出版品。如各科經典圖書、暢銷書、工具書等。
- 四、 應各學科教師之要求，需兩本以上之複本。

## Article 6 Principle of Duplicates

Using limited library funds to complete an extensive and effective collection, the collection of materials is based on a single volume. The following factors shall be taken into account if a duplicate copy is needed:

1. Academic and research value of the data.
2. Data Novelty.
3. Publications that are frequently borrowed, such as classic books in different subjects, bestsellers, and reference books.
4. More than two copies are required at the request of teachers of each subject.4

## 第三十四條 館藏淘汰

圖書館的主要目的，在於建立生動實用的館藏，因此需將不合時宜 的館藏淘汰。

- 一、 方式：依校內程序辦理資產減損，並附上「館藏淘汰減損 單」，經核決後，書目資料註銷登錄，館藏裝箱打包暫存備 查，再彙總委交總務處一併處理。
- 二、 優先順序：
- (一) 複本：利用價值不大者，只需淘汰剩二、三本即可。
- (二) 舊版資料：除具典藏價值外，當有新版可代替或已失參 考價值者，將予以淘汰。
- (三) 破損、老舊資料：破舊不堪使用之資料，如其內容尚屬 重要，儘量予以修補，或另備他本代替，否則予以淘 汰。

- (四) 學報：因館藏空間有限且學報於網路資源可檢索或透過 館際合作取得，除本校學報外皆予以淘汰。
- (五) 供播映非書資料之設備已經時代淘汰，或該資料已被其 他先進媒體儲存設備取代者，皆予以淘汰。

## 三、 期刊註銷淘汰原則：

中文現期期刊於出版隔年 12 月起得註銷淘汰，本校與相關機 構出版之期刊例外。外文現期期刊於出版隔年 12 月起有下列 情形之一者，得註銷淘汰。

- (一) 館藏卷期完整已裝訂後之多餘複本。
- (二) 休閒性或非學術性之期刊。
- (三) 期刊來源為贈閱，且無學術價值者。
- (四) 圖書館如已擁有可買斷之全文光碟資料庫或享有永久使 用保障之電子期刊，其相關之紙本期刊。

## Article 34 Collection Obsolescence

The main purpose of a library is to create vivid and useful collections; therefore, outdated collections shall be obsolete.

1. Method: Work on impairment of assets in accordance with the internal procedures of this School, to which the "Collection Obsolescence Impairment Form" is attached. After approval, the bibliographic data shall be logged out, the collection will be packed and temporarily stored for future reference, thereby being gathered, and handed over to the Office of General Affairs for disposition.

## 2. Priority:

- (1) Copies: The copies of little value shall be eliminated and only two or three can be kept.
- (2) Materials from Previous Editions: Shall be eliminated if they can be replaced by new editions or have lost their reference value, except for the materials of collection value.
- (3) Damaged and Old Materials: If the content of the dilapidated and unusable materials is still important, try best to repair it or replace it with another copy, otherwise it shall be eliminated.
- (4) Academic Journals: Since the collection space is limited and academic journals can be searched from online resources or acquired through inter-library cooperation, all academic journals shall be eliminated except this School's academic journals.
- (5) Equipment for playing non-book materials that has become obsolete or has been replaced by other advanced media storage equipment shall

be eliminated.

3. Principles for Cancellation and Obsolescence of Periodicals:

Current periodicals in Chinese may be canceled and obsolete starting from December of next year following the publication, except for the periodicals published by this School and related institutions. Current periodicals in foreign languages may be canceled and obsolete if they fall into any of the following circumstances starting from December of next year following the publication.

- (1) Copies in excess of the collected volumes that have been completely bound.
- (2) Recreational or non-academic periodicals.
- (3) The source of the periodical is given free by the publisher and has no academic values.
- (4) Hard copies of periodicals related to the library's full-text disc database that can be bought out or the electronic periodicals with a guaranteed benefit of permanent use.

- (三) 借書期限計算標準：
1. 借期包括星期日及例假日。
2. 借書期限自辦理借書之第二日起算至到期日止。
3. 借書逾期屢催不還，除照章罰款外，並視情形停止其借 書權。

- i. Retired faculty and staff.

- ii. Alumni (Need to produce a graduation certificate separately for such application)

- (1) For faculty members and students who are subject to the schoolleaving procedure, their library passes' numbers shall expire after the date they complete the school-leaving procedure.

- (1) When borrowing a book, the borrower shall produce his/her valid ID, and check out after being verified by the librarian and put into the computer system for borrowing and returning books.
- (2) When returning books, the borrowed books or data should be handed over to the circulation desk for check-in procedure.

4. Reference Rules in the Library
- (1) Circulating materials that cannot be borrowed include reference books designated by teachers, rare and precious books, academic dissertations, teachers' works, bound volumes of periodicals on fifth-floor, newspapers and uncatalogued new books, year books and other circulating materials of which the checkout is suspended due to special needs.

- (2) Circulating materials that cannot be borrowed are only for reference in the library without being lent out.
5. Reservation
- (1) If the books that the applicant wants to borrow have already been lent out, the applicant may go online or make a reservation at the circulation desk.
- (2) If the applicant makes a reservation for a book that he/she is reading, and there is another person who has made a reservation before the return date, such applicant's reservation order shall be the last.
- (3) If multiple people make reservations for the same material that is not being read by such people, the borrowing order shall be arranged based on the reservation time.
- (4) If the reserved materials have arrived at the library, the applicant who made the reservation shall go to the circulation desk to check out within seven days from the first issuance of the borrowing notice, otherwise it shall be deemed a waiver.
6. Library Renewal
- (1) If the borrower still needs to read the book before the expiration date, he/she may make a renewal once, except for audio-visual materials.
- (2) When making a renewal, the borrower shall bring his library pass to this Library in person to go through the renewal procedures or make a renewal online through this Library's website.
- (3) Circulating materials that are overdue or have been reserved or being asked to return may not be renewed.
7. Borrowing Period and Number of Books
- (1) For faculty and staff who are subject to the school-leaving procedure, the number of borrowed books including attachments is limited to twenty books for a period of 60 days, covering four audio-visual discs for a period of seven days and an electronic reading device as well as two interlibrary cooperative library passes for a period of 30 days, and periodicals for a period of seven days.
- (2) For part-time teachers, the number of borrowed books including attachments is limited to twenty books for a period of 60 days, covering four audio-visual discs for a period of seven days and an electronic reading device as well as two interlibrary cooperative library passes for a period of 30 days, and periodicals for a period of seven days.

- attachments is limited to twenty books for a period of 30 days, covering four audio-visual discs for a period of seven days and an electronic reading device as well as two interlibrary cooperative library passes for a period of 30 days, and periodicals for a period of seven days.
- (4) For undergraduate and junior college students, the number of borrowed books including attachments is limited to twenty books for a period of 30 days, covering four audio-visual discs for a period of seven days and an electronic reading device as well as two interlibrary cooperative library passes for a period of 30 days, and periodicals for a period of seven days.
- (5) For credit program students, the number of borrowed books is limited to five books, and an additional five volumes of book attachments, for a period of 30 days. Among them, the attachments include an access card for the third floor, which can be borrowed for thirty days.
- (6) For Formosa Plastics-related enterprises and long-term campus service workers, the number of borrowed books including attachments is limited to twenty books for a period of 60 days, covering two interlibrary cooperative library passes for a period of 30 days.
- (7) For retired faculty and staff, the number of borrowed books is limited to five books, and an additional five volumes of book attachments, for a period of 30 days.
- (8) For alumni, the number of borrowed books is limited to five books, and an additional five volumes of book attachments, for a period of 30 days. Among them, the attachments include an access card for the third floor, which can be borrowed for thirty days.
- (9) For manufacturers in the incubation center, the number of borrowed books is limited to five books, and an additional five volumes of book attachments, for a period of 30 days.
- (10) For research project assistants who are not current students at this School, the number of borrowed books is limited to five books, and an additional five volumes of book attachments, for a period of 30 days.
8. Overdue Fines
- (1) If the borrowed circulation materials are not returned at the end of the deadline, a daily fine of NT$5 per volume (piece) will be imposed, provided that no fine will be calculated for the library closed dates.
- (2) An Email for Library Courtesy Notice is only a reminder service, and the reader will not be fined based on whether the reader receives the

notice.

- (3) Calculation Criteria for Borrowing Period:
- i. Borrowing period includes Sundays and holidays.
- ii. Borrowing period starts from the next day when the book is borrowed and ends on the expiration date.
- iii. If a borrowed book is overdue and fails to be returned after repeated reminders, the borrower's right to borrow books may also be suspended depending on the circumstances, in addition to being fined according to regulations.
- iv. For an overdue return, an overdue fine is required to be paid at the same time, otherwise the right to borrow books will be suspended.
- v. The maximum fine for each transaction is NT$500.
- (4) For retired faculty and alumni's overdue return, failure to return the books four months overdue shall be deemed a deliberate overdue return, and the borrowing rights will be permanently revoked in addition to the foregoing penalty provisions.
9. Reminder
- (1) Due to overdue return or other needs, this Library may remind the borrower of overdue return at any time; failure to return books by the stipulated due date shall be deemed to be overdue.
- (2) When the faculty members and students who are subject to the schoolleaving procedure leave school, they shall return the borrowed books. Otherwise, the school-leaving procedure shall not be processed, and this Library will coordinate with the relevant divisions to continue to urge the students to return the books. The borrowers shall still be responsible for compensation.
10. Compensation

If the borrowed circulation materials are seriously defaced, damaged or lost due to the borrower's intention or negligence, the borrower shall compensate according to the following provisions:

- (1) Proactively purchase original circulation materials for Compensation. If the original circulation material is out of print, compensation shall be paid for a replaced circulation material that meets at least two of the three conditions with the same title, the same author, or the same publisher.
- (2) If the original circulation materials cannot be purchased, except for the public presentation edition of the audio-visual materials which shall be

- based on the fixed price, the rest shall be compensated at three times the fixed price.
- (3) For an electronic reading device, the borrower shall be responsible for repairing or purchasing new products and return to the library the value which the defective thing had or according to the fixed price. Compensation for original new products is limited to new products of the same brand and no lower than the specifications of the originally borrowed device.
11. Claiming that the book has not been borrowed or that the book has been returned
- (1) A reader who declares that he/she has not borrowed a book or has returned a book shall apply for access to the circulation desk monitor screen within ten days from the expiration date of checking out and checking in circulation information.
- (2) If the circulation information about the un-borrowed or returned books claimed by the reader is verified to be true and the reader makes an application within the specified period, the fine paid by the reader shall be refunded in full and the reader's violation record shall be deleted.

- 大、由上而下、由左至右順序排列。
- 二、 類號相同者，比對作者號，上架方式與上述相同。
- 三、 英文書的作者號以作者姓氏第一個字母順序排列，如 A132、 C256…等。
- 四、 上架時發現放置錯誤之圖書，隨時抽出歸回原位。
- 五、 同樣的圖書而有不相同的類號，取出交館員更正統一後歸架。
- 六、 破損之圖書隨時順手修補，嚴重者取出交館員處理。
- 七、 定時擦拭書架，清除灰塵，保持架上整潔。

## Article 44 Stack Shelving and Rearrangement

1. Books are shelved and partitioned into sections of Chinese, English and Japanese languages after classification. They are arranged according to the given class numbers, from small to large, from top to bottom, and from left to right.
2. If the class numbers are the same, compare the author numbers and shelve them in the same way as mentioned above.
3. Author numbers of English books are arranged in alphabetical order of the
4. authors' last names, such as A132, and C256.
4. The books which are found to have been wrongly placed when shelving shall be taken out and re-shelved in their proper places at any time.
5. The same books with different class numbers shall be taken out and submitted to the librarian for re-shelving after number correction and unification.
6. Damaged books shall be repaired without difficulty at any time. In serious situations, they shall be taken out and handed over to the librarian for processing.
7. Wipe the bookshelf regularly to remove dust and keep the shelves tidy.

## 第四十五條 分區自主盤點

- 一、 擬定分區盤點計劃，將圖書、圖書附件、學位論文、非書資 料、期刊合訂本，配合人力及業務量，每五年完成一次盤點。
- 二、 以「條碼資料蒐集器」，登錄分區內所有的館藏資料，轉檔到 系統上與館藏資料比對。49

- 三、 條碼不完全或有缺損者，以手寫登錄並抽出重新印製條碼後歸 位。
- 四、 徹底清理讀者借閱、行政、系所外借、及錯放位置等類別圖 書，以確保盤點資料的可靠性。
- 五、 異常館藏資料造冊存檔，連續三次盤缺之館藏經呈核後報廢註 銷。

## Article 45 Partitioned and Independent Inventory

1. Develop a partitioned inventory plan to complete an inventory of books, book attachments, academic dissertations, non-book materials, and bound periodicals every five years in line with manpower and volume of work.
2. Use the "Barcode Data Terminal Scanner" to log in all collection data within the partition and transfer the files to the system for comparison with the collection data.
3. If the barcode is incomplete or defective, log it in by hand, extract the barcode and reprint it before returning it to its original place.
4. Entirely clean up the categories of books borrowed by readers, administrative divisions, and departments/institutes, and misplaced books to ensure the reliability of inventory data.
5. Abnormal collection materials shall be documented and archived, and collections that have been missing for three consecutive times shall be written off as a loss after approval.

The use of the borrowed large audio-visual group viewing room is no more than four hours at a time in principle, and the usage time shall match the opening hours of the Audio-Visual Center; such restriction shall not apply to teachers' use for teaching, provided that, it shall match the opening hours of the library and shall be accompanied by the teacher.
