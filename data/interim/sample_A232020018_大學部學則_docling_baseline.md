## 第一篇 總則

## Part 1 General Provisions

<!-- formula-not-decoded -->

- Article 1 The Academic Regulations for Undergraduate Studies

(hereinafter referred to as "the Academic Regulations") of Ming Chi University of Technology (hereinafter referred to as "the University") is hereby established in accordance with the University Act, the Enforcement Rules of the University Act, the Degree Conferral Act, the Enforcement Rules of the Degree Conferral Act, and other relevant laws and regulations, which governs the student registration, and other matters relating to students of the University.

第二篇 大學部

## Part 2 Undergraduate Division

## Chapter 1 Admissions

- 第二條 本校於每學年之始，公開招考四年制各系組(學位學程)一 年級新生，其招生簡章另訂之。
- Article 2 The University shall recruit first year students for 4-year programs in all departments (Bachelor Program) at the beginning of each academic year upon approval of the proposed admission procedures. The admission brochure shall be stipulated separately.
- 第三條 凡具有下列資格之一，經入學考試錄取者，得入本校四年 制一年級就讀：
- 一、 公立或已立案之私立技術型高級中等學校畢業者 （包括普通型高級中等學校附設之專業群科畢業 者）。
- 二、 公立或已立案之私立普通型高級中等職業學校或综 合型高級中等學校畢業者。
- 三、 公立或已立案之私立高級中等學校附設之職業類科 補習學校結業，並取得主管教育行政機關所發資格 證明書者。
- 四、 持有國外高級職業學校畢業證件，經我國駐外單位 驗證屬實者。
- 五、 大陸來台設籍，持有大陸高級職業學校學歷證件， 經海峽交流基金會及主管教育行政機關審查認定 者。
- 六、合於同等學力報考規定者。
- Article 3 Applicants eligible for any of the following, who have been granted admission upon taking the entrance examination, may be admitted to the first year of the 4-year program of the University:
1. Graduates from public or registered private technical high schools (including graduates from professional

groups affiliated to ordinary high schools).

2. Those who have graduated from a public or registered private general senior secondary vocational school or a comprehensive senior secondary school.
3. Those who have graduated from a vocational tutoring school affiliated to a public or registered private senior high school and obtained a qualification certificate issued by the competent education administrative agency.
4. Those who hold graduation certificates from foreign senior vocational schools, which have been verified by Chinese units stationed abroad.
5. Those from mainland China who come to Taiwan to register, hold academic certificates from higher vocational schools in mainland China, and have been reviewed and recognized by the Straits Exchange Foundation and the competent education administrative agency.
6. Applicants who meet the requirements for equivalent academic ability to apply for the admission to the University.

第四條 (刪除)

Article 4 (deleted)

- 第五條 本校得依教育部有關規定招收境外生，其辦法另訂之。 本校得依教育部有關規定，與境外大學校院合作辦理學生 修讀雙聯學制學位，其辦法另訂之。
- Article 5 In accordance with relevant rules and regulations of the Ministry of Education, the University may accept international students, and the regulations governing such admission shall be stipulated separately.

In accordance with relevant rules and regulations of the Ministry of Education, the University may co-operate with overseas institutions of higher education to admit students for joint dual-degree programs, and the regulations governing such co-operation shall be stipulated separately.

- 第六條 凡經錄取新生應於規定日期至本校辦理入學報到手續，繳 驗有效之學歷(力)證件。因重病或特殊事故不能在當日來 校者，應事先檢具證明辦理請假，未經准假或逾期未辦理 者，取消其入學資格。

未能於當日繳驗學歷(力)證件者，經本校核准後，得先行 入學，但須於規定期間內補繳，逾期未辦理或經審核不合 者，取消入學資格。

- Article 6 Admitted students should go to the school on the specified date to complete the registration procedures and submit valid academic certificates (ability) for verification. Those who cannot come to school on that day due to serious illness or special accidents must obtain proof of leave in advance. Those who fail to apply for leave without approval or after the deadline will have their admission qualifications revoked.

Those who fail to submit their academic qualification certificates for verification on the same day may be enrolled in the school first after approval by the school, but they must re-issue within the specified period. Those who fail to do so after the deadline or fail to meet the requirements after review will have their admission qualifications revoked.

- 第七條 新生及轉學生至本校辦理入學手續前，有下列情形之一 者，新生應檢具相關有效證明文件，於註冊截止前，向本 校申請保留入學資格一年。經學校核准後，始可於次學年 度入學，保留入學資格期間，毋需繳納任何費用。
1. 因重病須長期療養，並持有衛生福利部中央健康保險 署特約區域醫院以上出具之診斷證明者，期間以一年 為原則。

2. 應徵服兵役並持有證明者，保留入學資格期限至服役 期滿。
3. 因懷孕、分娩或撫育三歲以下子女並持有證明者，得 於註冊開始前，向學校申請保留入學資格，入學資格 保留年限，得依其懷孕、分娩或撫育三歲以下子女之 需要申請。
4. 因家境清寒，持有鄉鎮市區公所以上出具之低收入戶 證明者，期間以一年為原則。
5. 參加「青年教育與就業儲蓄帳戶方案」之高級中等學 校畢業生考取本校後，申請保留入學資格或於入學後 申請休學，期間以三年為限，但不計入原定保留入學 資格或休學期間之計算。
5. Article 7 Before freshmen and transfer students go through the admission procedures at our school, if they have one of the following circumstances, freshmen shall present relevant valid documents and apply to the University for retention of admission for one year before the registration deadline. Upon approval of the school, students can be enrolled in the next academic year, and there is no fee for the period of retention.
1. For those who require long-term recuperation due to serious illness and hold a diagnosis certificate issued by a regional hospital or above authorized by the Central Health Insurance Agency of the Ministry of Health and Welfare, the period shall be one year in principle.
2. Those who are subject to military service and hold a certificate will have their enrollment qualifications retained until the expiration of their service period.
3. Students may apply to the University for deferred admission due to pregnancy, childbirth, or raising a child under the age of three with supporting documents prior to the beginning of the registration period, and the number of years of deferred admission

may be determined on the basis of the needs of such students.

4. For those who are from a poor family and hold a lowincome household certificate issued by a township or city government office, the period shall be one year in principle.
5. Graduates of senior secondary schools participating in the Youth Education and Employment Saving Accounts Program may apply for deferred admission or leave of absence upon admission to the University for a period of up to three years, but such deferred admission or leave of absence shall not be counted in the calculation of the original period of deferred admission or leave of absence.
3. 第八條 新生及轉學生入學報到時，須繳驗有效之學歷或相關證 件，方得入學。如有正當理由，預先申請延期補繳而經核 准者，得先行入學，但應於規定期間內補繳，否則取消其 入學資格。
4. Article 8 New students and transfer students are required to submit valid academic qualifications or relevant documents when they register for admission. Should there be any valid reasons to apply for postponement of submission in advance, students may be admitted to the University as soon as their applications have been approved. However, the submission shall be made within the stipulated period, otherwise the students' eligibility for admission shall be cancelled.
5. 第九條 學生姓名、出生年月日，應以身分證所載為準。入學資格 證件所載與身分證所載不符合，應即提出更正。
6. Article 9 Students' names and dates of birth shall be as stated in their national identity cards. Any discrepancy between the information contained in the admission documents and that

in the national identity card shall be promptly corrected.

- 第十條 新生及轉學生入學考試如有舞弊或所繳入學學歷(力)或相 關證件有偽造、假借、冒用、塗改等情事，一經查明屬實 即撤銷錄取資格或開除學籍，且不發給任何相關修業證明 文件。如在本校畢業後始被察覺者，除依法撤銷其畢業資 格，並繳銷其學位證書。
- Article 10 New students and transfer students who have been found to have committed cheating or malpractice in the admission examination or whose documents of academic qualifications (ability) or related certificates have been forged, borrowed, fraudulently used, or altered, or with dual student status shall have their admission qualification revoked and be expelled from the University immediately upon conviction. Students shall not receive any certificates except for notification to their parents or guardians. In case the violation is revealed after graduation, in addition to revoking their graduation qualifications in accordance with the law, their degree certificates shall also be revoked.

## 第二章 繳費、註冊、選課

## Chapter 2 Payment of Fees, Registration and Course Selection

- 第十一條 學生應依規定日期辦理註冊。如因重病、特殊事故或因懷
- 孕、分娩或撫育三歲以下子女引發之事(病)假、產假，經 檢具證明文件於事前請假核准者，得延期註冊，但至多以 兩星期為限。

未經請假或超過准假日期未註冊者，新生如未報准保留入 學資格者則取消入學資格，舊生如未申請休學者即令退

學。

- Article 11 Students shall report to the University for registration on the specified date. If a student takes sick leave or maternity leave due to serious illness, special accidents, pregnancy, childbirth, or caring for children under three years old, and

obtains proof of leave approval before the application, the registration may be deferred for a period of no more than two weeks.

Students without approved excused leave or fail to complete the registration process within the approved leave period or who fail to apply for detention or deferred admission shall be disqualified from admission as new students, and existing students shall be subject to dismissal from the University.

- 第十二條 學生於每學期註冊時，應依照規定繳納各項費用。學生註 冊入學後申請休學或退學者，其退費標準依照教育部之規 定辦理。
- Article 12 Students shall pay all fees in accordance with rules and regulations when registering for each semester. Those who apply for suspension or withdrawal from the University after registration shall be refunded in accordance with the guidelines set forth by the Ministry of Education.
- 第十三條 凡已屆役齡或已服完兵役之學生，應於第一學期或新生註 冊截止前，檢具相關證明文件，依據本校學生辦理兵役緩 徵（召）相關規定申辦。
- Article 13 Students who have reached the age of military service or have completed their military service shall present the relevant documents and apply for military service deferment in accordance with the regulations of the University's regulations as stated in the Notice for Students Applying for Military Service Deferment in the first semester of study or before the deadline for new student enrollment.
- 第十四條 學生選課須依照規定課程表及本校選課須知辦理，並由學 生上網選課。凡已修習及格之科目，不得重選，隨班重 （補）修之科目，應於選課時一併辦理。

- Article 14 Students shall select courses in accordance with the prescribed curriculum and the Course Selection Instructions of the University, and students shall select courses online. Students are not allowed to re-take the courses they have already passed, and the courses that require retaking (remedial study) shall be handled at the same time as the course selection.
- 第十五條 學生每學期修習學分數不得多於廿七學分，惟三年級第二 學期不得多於三十二學分，一至二年級不得少於十六學 分，三至四年級不得少於九學分。

學生前學期學業平均成績在 A-以上，且名次在該系組(學 位學程)該年級學生數前百分之十以內者，次學期經系(學 程)主任核可後，得加選一至二科目之學分，並得修習較 高年級之必、選修課程。修習學分數上限不受前項限制。

就學役男若因申請就學期間服役或其他特殊情況，於當學 期擬修學分數高於第一項規定者，依本校相關規定並經系 (學程)主任與院長同意後，呈教務長核定，則不在此限。 另於原訂修業期限內超修之學分數則不予收取學分費。本 校「學士班學生就學期間服役彈性修業實施要點」另訂 之，並報教育部備查。

至境外交換學生於交換期間不受最低修習學分數之限制， 惟每學期(學季)至少應修二門科目。

- Article 15 Students shall not take more than 27 credits in each semester, except that the credits shall not be more than 32 credits second in the second semester of the third year, first and second-year students shall not take less than 16 credits, and third and fourth-year students shall not take less than 9 credits.

Students who have achieved an average of 80 points or Grade A- or above in the previous semester and whose rank is within the top 10% of the students in the department (Bachelor Program) group, may take extra credits in one to two courses as well as required and elective courses of senior years in the subsequent semester with the approval of the chair of their department (Bachelor Program). The maximum number of credits to be taken is not subject to the limitations of the preceding paragraph.

This doesn't apply where a serving male student is required to serve in the military while he is studying or due to other special circumstances, and the number of credits he intends to take in the current semester is higher than that specified in item 1, he shall submit it to the provost for approval in accordance with the relevant regulations of the school and with the consent of the department (study program) director and dean. In addition, no credit fee will be charged for the number of credits overtaken within the original study period. Our school's "Regulations on Students' Flexible Study of Bachelor's Degree During Military Service" is separately formulated and reported to the Ministry of Education for future reference.

Exchange students overseas are not subject to the minimum number of credits required during the exchange period, but they are required to take at least two subjects per semester (quarter).

- 第十六條 學生加、退選科目，應於每學期規定期限內辦理完成，經 系(學程)主任核准後送教務處登記，逾期不予受理。學生 未按規定辦理加、退選手續，學分概不承認。且不得因 加、退選科目而使其應修學分數超過或少於前條之規定。 學生於課程加退選截止後，因故無法繼續修習課程，得依 規定時程內申請停修課程，本校「學生申請期中停修課程 實施要點」另訂之。
- Article 16 Students who intend to add or drop any course shall complete the procedure within the prescribed period of each semester and send it to the Office of the Registrar for registration upon approval by the chair of their department

(Bachelor Program), after which the application shall not be accepted. In case students fail to complete the required procedures, the credits concerned shall not be recognized, and the grade of the courses dropped by them shall be counted as zero. Students may not add or drop a course to make their number of credits exceed or falls below the provisions of the preceding article.

After the deadline for course additions and withdrawals, students who are unable to continue taking the course for some reason may apply to discontinue the course within the prescribed time limit. Our school's "Directives Governing the Implementation of Students' Application for Discontinuation of Courses during the Semester" are separately formulated.

- 第十七條 學生不得修習上課時間互相衝突之科目，否則衝堂之各科 目概予註銷。
- Article 17 Students shall not take courses that clash with each other, otherwise these courses shall be dropped.
- 第十八條 各系組(學位學程)學生經核准得選修輔修系組(學位學程)。

選讀輔系者，至少應修畢輔系組(學位學程)專業（門）必 修科目二十學分；選定雙主修者，應修畢另一主修學系全 部專業（門）必修科目及學分，本校「學生選讀輔修科系 組辦法」及「學生修讀雙主修辦法」另訂之，並報教育部 備查。

- 第七十三條 授予之學位，如有入學資格或修業情形不實或舞弊情事， 經調查屬實者，應予撤銷，並公告註銷其已發之學位證書 後，應通知當事人繳還該學位證書，並將撤銷與註銷事 項，通知其他大專校院及相關機關（構）；其有違反其他
- 法令者，並應依相關法令處理。
- Article 73 If there is any false admission qualification or study status or cheating, such degree shall be revoked upon investigation and substantiation. After the announcement of the revocation of the issued degree certificate, the recipient concerned shall be notified to return his/her degree certificate, and such revocation shall also be made known to other academic institutions and related agencies (organizations). Any violation of applicable laws and regulations shall be handled in accordance with such laws and regulations.

## Part 4 Student Registration Management

- 第七十五條 學生在校肄業之系組(學位學程)班別、年級、成績，以及 註冊、轉學、休學、復學、退學等學籍記錄，悉以教務處 或進修推廣處教務組各項學籍與成績登記原始表冊為準。

- 第七十七條 學生於肄業期間出境，有關學業與學籍之處理，依本校 「學生出國期間有關學業及學籍處理要點」之規定辦理， 其要點另訂之。
- Article 77 Students who go abroad during their period of being absent from school shall follow the regulations of the University's Directives for Handling Students' Academic Affairs and

Academic Registrations during the Period of Departure from the Republic of China, which shall be stipulated separately.

- 第七十八條 本校應於每學年（期）開始後二個月內造具各系組(學位 學程)新生、轉學生名冊。具有保留入學資格者，另附名 冊置校內永久保存。
- Article 78 A register of new students and transfer students of departments (Bachelor Program), as well as detailed statistical reports, shall be compiled within two months of the commencement of each semester. For students who are eligible for deferred admission, a separate register shall be created and permanently archived at the University.
- 第七十九條 畢業生資格由本校依規定自行審核，於畢業生畢業後四個 月內，造具畢業生學位名冊由學校永久保存。
- Article 79 The qualification of graduates shall be reviewed by the University in accordance with regulations. A register of graduates shall be compiled within four months after their graduation and permanently archived at the University.
- 第八十條 本校應於次學期開始後二個月內，造具退學學生名冊置學 校永久保存。
- Article 80 A register of students who have withdrawn from the University shall be compiled within two months after the commencement of the subsequent semester and shall be permanently archived at the University.
