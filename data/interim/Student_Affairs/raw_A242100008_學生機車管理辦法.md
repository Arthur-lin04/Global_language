## 學生機車管理辦法 Regulations on Management of Student Motorcycles

89年05月15日校務會議制訂

114年08月19日學生事務會議修訂

Amended passed by the Student Affairs Meeting on May 15, 2000. Amendment passed by the Student Affairs Meeting on August 19, 2025.

## 第一條 目的

本校為維護校園安寧、交通安全、環境景觀及停車秩序， 依本校「車輛管理辦法」訂定「學生機車管理辦法」（以 下簡稱本辦法），適用對象大學日間部及研究所學生。

## Article 1 Purpose

To maintain campus tranquility, traffic safety, environmental aesthetics, and parking order, the University hereby establishes the "Regulations on Management of Student Motorcycles" (hereinafter referred to as "these Regulations"), in accordance with the University's "Vehicle Management Measures". These Regulations apply to undergraduate and graduate students.

## 第二條 管理原則

- 一、本校學生每學年開學後應依本辦法申領「機車停車 證」，並依規定繳納停車場場地清潔費，發給「機車 停車證」。
- 二、機車停車區， A、(近綜合大樓)、B(近創新大樓)、 C(近綠能中心)等三區，機車應停放於原始申請指定
- 之停車區內，不可跨區停放。
- 三、申請機車停車證之機車，未經核准不得進入校園。
- 四、機車停車證應黏貼於機車後方擋泥板上以供查驗，未 有擋泥板之機車，不得申請停車證。騎乘機車應遵守

1

- 相關交通法規，出入停車場及校園時應接受保全人員 及學校人員之查驗。
- 五、本校停車場僅供機車停放，不負機車保管及損壞、遺
- 失責任。

## Article 2 Management Principles

1. Each academic year, students shall apply for a "Motorcycle Parking Permit" under these Regulations and pay the designated parking space cleaning fee to obtain the permit.
2. Motorcycle parking areas are divided into three zones: A (near the General Building), B (near the Innovation Building) and C (near the Battery Research Center). Motorcycles must be parked within the designated zone specified in the original application and may not be parked across zones.
3. Motorcycles applying for parking permits must be approved before entering the campus.
4. The motorcycle parking permit must be affixed to the rear mudguard of the motorcycle for inspection. Motorcycles without mudguards are not eligible to apply for parking permits. Riders must comply with relevant traffic regulations and undergo inspection by security personnel and school staff when entering and leaving parking lots and campus.
5. The University parking facilities are for motorcycle parking only. The University does not assume responsibility for the custody, damage, loss, or theft of motorcycles.

第三條 學生申請「機車停車證」規定如下：

- 一、學生「機車停車證」以每學年辦理一次為原則，機車 停放清潔費每學年為 500 元，下學期因需求申辦「機 車停車證」者，收場地清潔費300元。
- 二、辦理機車停車場清潔費退費，以學期為單位計，得退 費半價為250元，第二學期開學後不予退費。
- 三、申請人應備妥校園卡、駕駛執照、行車執照、保險證 等影本及場地清潔費繳費收據，並填具「機車停車證 申請書」至生輔組辦理。
- 四、領有身心殘障手冊(屬於肢體殘障行動不便類別)及符 合全民健保重大傷病標準之學生，得向生輔組申請 「全學期通行證」。
- 五、學生機車若因故（公務或因傷不良於行等）須進入校 區者，應向生輔組（社團活動向課外組）申請學生車 輛臨時通行證。
- 六、學生申請「機車停車證」須參加校內辦理經認可之交 通安全講習，未曾參加者，不予申請。

Article 3 Regulations for Student Application for "Motorcycle Parking Permit"

1. The "Motorcycle Parking Permit" is processed once per academic year. The motorcycle parking cleaning fee is 500 NTD per academic year. Students who apply for the "Motorcycle Parking Permit" in the second semester due to demand will be charged a cleaning fee of 300 NTD.
2. Motorcycle parking cleaning fee refunds are processed on a semester basis. A refund of half the fee, amounting to 250 NTD, can be granted. No refund will be provided after the second semester begins.
3. Applicants must prepare a copy of their campus card, driver's license, vehicle registration, insurance certificate,

- and the payment receipt for the parking cleaning fee, and complete the "Motorcycle Parking Permit Application Form" for processing at the Student Discipline Division.
4. Students holding a disability handbook (for physical disability or mobility impairment) or meeting the standards for major illnesses under the National Health Insurance criteria may apply for a "Full Semester Pass" from the Student Discipline Division.
5. Students whose motorcycles must enter the campus for reasons such as official business or medical conditions (e.g., injuries) may apply for a temporary vehicle pass from the Student Discipline Division (or the Division of Extracurricular Activity for club-related activities).
6. Students applying for the "Motorcycle Parking Permit" must attend a recognized traffic safety seminar conducted on campus. Applications will not be processed for those who have not attended the seminar.

## 第四條 補發規定

個人遺失、損毀「機車停車證」或需換證時，應繳回原有 停車證（遺失、損毀時應至生輔組填具切結書並繳交工本 費100元），由生輔組補發停車證。

## Article 4 Reissuance Regulations

If an individual loses or damages their "Motorcycle Parking Permit" or requires a replacement, they must return the original permit (in the case of loss or damage, the individual must fill out a declaration form at the Student Discipline Division and pay a processing fee of 100 NTD). The Student Discipline Division will then issue a replacement permit.

- 第五條 停車場開放時間 各區機車停車場 24 小時開放使用並設有自動柵欄機管制 機車進出，使用者須申請機車停車證並憑「校園卡」或「員 工卡」、「臨時卡」感應進出。
- Article 5 Parking Lot Opening Hours

Motorcycle parking lots in all zones are open 24 hours a day and are equipped with automatic gate control systems for motorcycle entry and exit. Users must apply for a motorcycle parking permit and use their "Campus Card," "Employee Card," or "Temporary Card" for entry and exit.

## 第六條 違規處理

- 一、機車違規進入校園、騎機車未載安全帽及違規騎乘機 車者，依學生獎懲規定著予記「申誡乙次」處分，且 禁止機車及所有人當學期內不可再申請臨時機車通 行證入校園行駛。
- 二、未申請「機車停車證」或未粘貼機車後方擋泥板上之 本校核准當學年有效之「機車停車證」，而停放於本 校機車停車場或進入校園者，將逕行鎖車罰款，情節 重大者，外加拖吊處理。
- 三、凡年度有違規遭舉發、鎖車及處分者，需重新參加新 學年度校內辦理經認可之交通安全講習，未參加者，
- 不予申請機車停車證。
- 四、依「車輛管理辦法」機車違規停放經取締上鎖後處理 原則辦理。

## Article 6 Violations and Penalties

1. Students who violate campus regulations by entering the campus with a motorcycle, riding without a helmet, or

- riding in violation of traffic rules will receive a "warning" as per the Student Disciplinary Regulations. Additionally, the student and their motorcycle will be prohibited from applying for a temporary motorcycle pass for the remainder of the semester.
2. Students who fail to apply for a "Motorcycle Parking Permit" or fail to affix the approved, valid permit on the rear mudguard of their motorcycle while parking in the University's motorcycle parking lots or entering the campus will have their motorcycles locked and fined. For serious violations, towing may also be implemented.
3. Students who are cited for violations, have their motorcycles locked, or face penalties during the year must attend a recognized traffic safety seminar for the new academic year. Failure to attend will result in a denial of the "Motorcycle Parking Permit" application.
4. Violations of motorcycle parking as stipulated in the "Vehicle Management Measures" will be handled according to the principles for enforcement and lock-up procedures.
