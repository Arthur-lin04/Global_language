## 第一條 依據

為處理學生之課程學分抵免，依據本校大學部學則第四十條訂定 「學生抵免學分辦法」（以下簡稱本辦法）。

## Article 1 Basis

The "Regulations for Credit Transfer and Course Waiver of Students" (hereinafter called these "Regulations") is established in accordance with Article 40 of MUCT's Undergraduate Program Regulations to handle credit transfer and course waiver of students.

## 第二條 適用對象

- 一、 轉系（組）生、新生、轉學生。
- 二、 法令准許先修讀學分後考取修讀學位者。
- 三、 申請放棄選讀輔系學生。
- 四、 申請放棄雙主修，且已修學分未達選讀輔系標準之學生。
- 五、 依照法令規定於國內外教育部認可之大學或獨立學院獲得 推廣教育學分後，考取本校之新生
- 六、 依本校「學生出境期間有關學業及學籍處理要點」規定於 國外大學或獨立學院取得學分者。
- 七、 曾於大學或獨立學院三年級以上或技術學院二年制肆業， 但未取得該校畢業證書，重新錄取本校之新生。
- 八、 經本校核准修讀雙聯學制並持有修習及格之科目及學分證
- 明者。
- 九、 新舊課程交替之學生。

## Article 2 Applicability

1. These Regulations shall apply to department (section) transfer students, new students, and transferred students.
2. These Regulations also apply to: Students with credits earned prior to enrolling to the current degree program as approved by law.
3. Students waiving their minor programs.
4. Students waiving their second major programs and the credits earned are below the standard as a minor.
5. New students enrolling at MCUT after earning credits from continuing education courses taken from a domestic or overseas university or independent college accredited by the Ministry of Education (MOE) by law.
6. Students with credits earned from overseas universities or independent colleges according to MCUT's "Directions for Handling the Study and Status of Students Outside Taiwan."
7. New MCUT students who were university or college dropouts in the third year or dropouts of two-year technical programs.
8. Students who are approved by the University to pursue a joint dual-degree program and possess certification of passed courses and credits.
9. Students switching from the previous curriculum to the current curriculum.
10. Students with credits, results above B-, earned from a doctoral and/or master's program(s) when enrolling for a bachelor's or master's program without including in the graduation credits for the bachelor's or master's program.

## 第三條 抵免學分數

新生辦理抵免之科目，應以該生入學年度之課程總表之科目為 準，復學生復學前已修過學分數者，以該生入學年度之課總表之 科目為準，若是尚未修過學分數者，以相銜接之年級課程總表為 準；轉系（組）生則以轉入年級之課程總表為辦理抵 免之依據。

申請放棄輔系、雙主修學生，得申請已修過之科目學分抵免主修 系（組）不足之選修學分，惟不得超過本校選課辦法之規定。

本校依所申請抵免科目之優先順序酌予抵免，惟抵免總學分數不 得超過各系規定最低畢業學分三分之一，於本校取得學分數則不 得超過各系規定最低畢業學分二分之一。學生具預研生身份者（含 學碩士3+2交換生、碩博士1+3交換生），於本校取得學分數不得 超過各系規定最低畢業學分三分之二。

大學部學生經核准抵免之總學分數超過該年級應修學分者，得申 請提高編級，重新編入適當年級就讀，但至少應在校修業滿一學 年。核准提高編級後，學生適用之課程規劃及畢業條件均依照編 級後之年級為準始可畢業，惟修業年限不因此而延長。

大學部抵免學分說明：

- 一、 抵免畢業總學分數四分之一以上者可申請編高為二年級。
- 二、 抵免畢業總學分數四分之二以上者可申請編高為三年級。
- 三、 抵免畢業總學分數四分之三以上者可申請編高為四年級。

## Article 3 Number of Credits for Transfer

The courses allowed for credit transfer for new students shall be subject to the courses as listed in the general course list in based on the curriculum of the academic year of admission. For returning students, credits already completed prior to re-enrollment shall follow the curriculum plan of their original admission year; for courses not yet completed, the curriculum plan of the corresponding continuing grade shall apply. For students transferring between departments (or programs), the curriculum plan of the grade into which they are admitted shall serve as the

basis for credit transfer.

Students waiving a minor or second major program may request that credits previously earned be applied toward elective credits required by their major program. without exceeding the limit as stated in the guidelines for course selection However, the total number of credits applied in this manner shall not exceed the limits established by the university's course registration regulations.

MCUT shall determine credit transfers according to the courses prioritized by students based on the prioritized courses applied by students. However, the total number of credits allowed for transfer transferred credits shall not exceed one-third of the minimum graduation credit requirements of each department. Credits earned at MCUT shall not exceed one-half of the minimum graduation credit requirements of each department. For students with pre-admission status (including 3+2 program students, 1+3 program students), the total shall not exceed two-thirds of the minimum graduation credits required by each department.

Students whose approved transferred credits exceed two-thirds of the required credits for their current year level, or who are unable to meet the minimum enrollment credit requirements stipulated by academic regulations, may apply for advancement to a higher year level upon approval. They will be reassigned to a suitable year level for continued study. However, they must complete at least one academic year at the university and fulfill the minimum graduation credit requirements of their department to graduate. The duration of study shall not be extended as a result.

Undergraduate Credit Transfer Guidelines:

1. Students who transfer credits amounting to at least one-quarter of the total graduation credits may apply for advancement to the second year
2. Students who transfer credits amounting to at least one-half of the total graduation credits may apply for advancement to the third year.
3. Students who transfer credits amounting to at least three-quarters of the total graduation credits may apply for advancement to the fourth year.

## 第四條 抵免原則

科目抵免原則

科目之抵免包括必修與選修科目。

學分抵免原則

- 一、 科目名稱及內容均相同者。
- 二、 科目名稱不同而內容相同者。
- 三、 科目名稱及內容不同但實質內容相同者。（僅適用於選修 學分）。

- 不同學分之科目相互抵免原則
- 一、 以多抵少者：抵免後以較少學分登記。惟因本校新舊課 程交替，多餘之學分經系主任同意得抵免該系專業選修。
- 二、 以少抵多者：本校若有相關科目可補修該科目不足之學 分，准予於修畢該補修科目後抵免；本校若無相關科目 可補修該科目不足之學分時，不准抵免。
- 三、 113-114學年課程內容及學時不變但提高學分數之科目，且 經教務會議專案審查通過者，不受前款之限制。

## Article 4 Principles of Course Waiver and Credit Transfer

## Principles of course waiver

The courses allowed for waiver shall include both required and elective courses.

## Principles of credit transfer

1. Courses with the same name and contents.
2. Courses with different names but the same contents.
3. Courses with different titles and descriptions but substantially similar content. (Applicable to elective credit only)

Principles for waiver of courses with different credits

1. Waiver of courses with more credits for courses with less credits: Register the course with less credits after the waiver. However, the surplus credits after switching from the previous curriculum to the current curriculum can be transferred to the department's professional electives with the approval of the department chair.
2. Waiver of courses with less credits for courses with more credits: When there are relevant courses with credits that can make up the credit difference at MCUT, students can excuse a course with more credits after completing a relevant course. If no relevant courses are available at MCUT, no waiver of course shall be allowed.
3. The subjects for the academic year 2024-2025, where the course content and hours remain unchanged but the credit are increased, and which have been approved by the Academic Council Meeting through special review, are not subject to the

restrictions of the previous clause.

## 第五條 抵免申請

符合第二條適用對象規定應於每學期加退選結束前，填具「學分 抵免申請書」（表號：A232060213），檢附歷年成績證明與課程

綱要證明直接向所屬各系提出學分抵免之申請，以一次為限。

大學部四年制學生畢業門檻之抵免，依本校「學生畢業門檻實施 辦法」辦理。

- Article 5 Application for Credit Transfer and Course Waiver

Students eligible for credit transfer and course waiver under Article 2 shall fill in the Application for Credit Transfer and Course Waiver (Form: A232060213) and submit the form together with the academic transcript over the years and course syllabus certificate to apply for credit transfer and course waiver to their departments before the closing date of course addition and dropping each semester. Only one application for credit transfer and course waiver shall be allowed.

The transfer of graduation credit threshold of 4-year undergraduate programs shall be subject to MCUT's Regulations for Implementation of Graduation Threshold.

## 第六條 國外修課學分與成績登錄處理原則

- 一、 學期制(Semester System)之學校，依規定承認其學分，學季 制(Quarter System)之學校，其學分時數應以授課18小時為1 學分為原則轉換為本校學分。
- 二、 非百分制成績得依原校提供之成績轉換為等第制對照表後 登錄；原校未能提供轉換說明者，則依照本校之附表轉換。
- 三、 學生出國修課之學分，以本附表為轉換原則，若有個別認 定之情況，學生需檢附學分、修課時數及成績相關證明文 件辦理。
- Article 6 Regulations for the Transfer of Credits and Grade Registration for Courses Taken Abroad
1. For schools adopting the Semester System, credits are recognized according to the regulations. For schools under the Quarter System, credits shall be converted based on the principle that 18 hours of instruction equals one credit at this

university.

2. Non-percentage grades may be recorded after being converted into a letter grade system according to the grading conversion table provided by the original institution. If the original institution does not provide a conversion explanation, the university's conversion table will be applied.
3. Credits earned from courses taken abroad shall be converted based on this table. In cases requiring individual assessment, students must submit relevant documents, including proof of credits, course hours, and grades.

## 第七條 科目替代

學生於入學後如有科目補修、必修科目不及格重修時，若遇原課 程消失或大四以上學生遇該課程衝堂無法修習時，得以原教學單 位為優先或輔以其他教學單位（含他校）之學分，依抵免原則申 請替代，填具「科目替代申請單」（表號：A232060313），檢附 課程綱要或教學進度表向所屬教學單位提出申請。

## Article 7 Subject substitution

After enrollment, if students need to retake failed compulsory subjects or make up for other subjects, and if the original course is no longer offered or there are scheduling conflicts that prevent senior students (in their fourth year or above) from taking the course, they may prioritize credits from the original teaching unit or alternatively use credits from other teaching units (including those from other institutions) as a substitute based on the exemption application. They should fill out the "Subject Substitution Application Form" (form number: A232060310), attach the course syllabus or teaching schedule, and submit the application to the affiliated teaching unit.

## 第八條 審核方式

學分抵免之初審，通識課程由通識教育中心負責，專業科目則由 學生所屬之各系負責。初審完畢後，由教務處註冊組、進修學院 教務組進行複審並通知學生抵免情形。

## Article 8 Evaluation Methods

The General Education Center shall take charge of the preliminary evaluation of credit transfer for general education courses, while each department shall take charge of the preliminary evaluation of credit transfer for department professional courses. After the preliminary evaluation, the Registry Division of the Office of Academic Affairs and the Division of Academic Affairs of the Office of Continuing and Extension Education shall take charge of the second evaluation and notify students of the transfer and waiver results.

## 第九條 注意事項

- 一、 抵免後之科目，其原有成績不計入本校學生之學期成績， 僅於各項成績登記文件中註明「抵免」。
- 二、 不論學分抵免多寡，學生各學期所修學分數不得低於本校 學則之規定。
- 三、 必修科目停開、更改名稱或更改學分數時，各系及通識中 心得選定性質相近之科目，供新舊課程交替重(補)修之學生 修習；惟學生之畢業總學分數，仍應按本校學則之規定， 不得減少。

## Article 9 Dos and Don'ts

1. After a course waiver, the score of the waived (exempted) course will not be included in the score of the semester but only marked as "waiver" in the transcript.
2. Regardless of the credits from course waiver, students shall meet the minimum credit requirements for each semester according to the MCUT School Regulations.
3. When a required course is canceled or renamed or its number of credits is changed, departments and the General Education Center may select courses of similar nature for repeat (make-up) students. However, the credit requirements for graduation shall still be subject to the MCUT School Regulations without reduction.

## 第十條 相關規定

本辦法如有未盡事宜，悉依本校學則及相關規定辦理。

## Article 10 Unsettled Matters

Matters not provided for in these Regulations shall be subject to the MCUT School Regulations and the relevant regulations.

## 第十一條 實施與修訂

本辦法經教務會議通過，陳校長核定後公布實施，修訂時亦同。

## Article 11 Implementation and Amendment

These Regulations shall be promulgated and implemented after the passage of the Academic Council Meeting and the approval of the president. The same shall apply to the amendments hereto.
